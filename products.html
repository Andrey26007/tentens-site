Yes. Every product works on its own. Start with the one that solves your most urgent problem and add more — or a team — when you need it.

We begin with a short discovery of your architecture and integrations, and agree on the integration path before anything goes live.

Yes. The people who built the product can join as a team extension or run the integration as a fixed-scope project.

How do you handle security and data?

Payment systems follow the PCI DSS standard. We are happy to discuss NDAs and your security requirements before you share any details.
